I'm happy to share that I have been awarded the **NTHU Emmanuel O. Salawu Scholarship for the 2026–2027 academic year (115學年度)**.

The **College of Life Sciences and Medicine, National Tsing Hua University**, announced the award on October 8, 2026, recognizing me as a PhD student in the Institute of Bioinformatics and Structural Biology.
