**Research project:** Using Steered Molecular Dynamics to Study the −1 Programmed Ribosomal Frameshifting Mechanism of SARS-CoV-2.

This project is part of my PhD research in the Computational Biology Laboratory at National Tsing Hua University. I use steered molecular dynamics to investigate the structural dynamics associated with −1 programmed ribosomal frameshifting in SARS-CoV-2.

**Research areas:** Computational biology, molecular simulation, and RNA structural dynamics.

**Supervisor:** Prof. Lee-Wei Yang.
