**Research project:** Acid-Enriched Unfolding Intermediates of a pH-Sensitive Pseudoknot Enhances Ribosomal Frameshifting.

This project examines the relationship between pH-sensitive RNA pseudoknot unfolding and ribosomal frameshifting. It forms part of my PhD research in the Computational Biology Laboratory at National Tsing Hua University.

**Research areas:** RNA structural biology, molecular biophysics, and ribosomal frameshifting.

**Manuscript:** “Acid-Enriched Unfolding Intermediates of a pH-Sensitive Pseudoknot Enhances Ribosomal Frameshifting Efficiency” — in preparation, as listed in my CV.

**Supervisor:** Prof. Lee-Wei Yang.
