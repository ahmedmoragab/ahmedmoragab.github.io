Today was Run 52 of my 100-day 5 km challenge, and it became a milestone I had been working toward for a long time: my first official **under-30-minute 5 km run**. I covered **5.03 km in 30:02**, averaging **5:58 min/km**.

The timing made the achievement even more special. I had run my first half marathon the day before, so setting a new 5K personal record on the following day felt like an incredible continuation of that breakthrough. It was a reminder that one memorable effort can create momentum for the next one.

![Day 52 — Sub-30-minute 5K activity summary]({{ page.photo | relative_url }})

## My Run

- **Date:** November 4, 2026, at 19:24
- **Location:** Xiangshan District, Hsinchu
- **Distance:** 5.03 km
- **Moving time:** 30:02
- **Average pace:** 5:58 min/km
- **Elevation gain:** 10 m
- **Calories:** 581 kcal
- **Average heart rate:** 148 bpm
- **Daily goal:** 5 km — met!

This is my first official sub-30-minute 5K, and it is now one of the defining moments of this challenge. After the half marathon, I was especially happy to see that the effort had not only been a one-day peak: I could come back the next day and make another record.

After this run, the 100-day challenge stands at **52 completed runs over 53 elapsed days**, for a **98.11% consistency score**.
