On paper, this was my **second-fastest 5 km run**, just six seconds shy of my best recorded time. But to me, it felt like an unofficial personal best!

I ran during rush hour and had to stop several times for traffic. The weather was hotter than I expected, too. Despite all that, the app recorded **5.00 km in 30:17**.

I honestly believe that without those interruptions, I could have finished **under 30 minutes**. I can't call it an official sub-30 yet, but today's effort makes me feel that it's within reach. 😄

Now I'm even more motivated to try again over the coming days and see that sub-30 finish recorded in the app!
